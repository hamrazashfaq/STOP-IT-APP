# Religious content references

The app intentionally includes only a small amount of fixed, source-cited material. Text below is not generated dynamically.

## Quran

- Surah Al-`Alaq 96:14 (Arabic and English): [Quran.com 96:14](https://quran.com/96/14). English string used: “Does he not know that Allah sees?” (Saheeh International).
- Urdu wording is credited in-app to Muhammad Junagarhi. See the translation listing for Al-`Alaq 96:14 at [Al-Hadees Quran translations](https://al-hadees.com/alquran-translations/96/14). The app's Urdu translation is a faithful short rendering; it is not labeled as Saheeh International (which is an English translation).

## Hadith

- Sahih al-Bukhari 6114, narrated by Abu Huraira: “The strong is not the one who overcomes the people by his strength, but the strong is the one who controls himself while in anger.” [Sunnah.com Bukhari 6114](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6114).
- The related narration appears as Sahih Muslim 2609; [Sunnah.com Muslim 2609](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2609). Arabic is displayed with the English rendering; Urdu is an explanatory rendering, not presented as a separate canonical translation.

Before distribution, independently re-check the text, exact numbering, translation edition, and applicable text-use rights with a knowledgeable reviewer. Do not add AI-generated religious quotations.
